Eden Asipov

Maintenance Analyst · Boeing

Eden began her aerospace career at SpaceX at 17, then started at Amazon Leo (formerly Project Kuiper) on her 18th birthday.

She went on to work as a contract technical writer at Virgin Galactic, and is now a full-time Maintenance Analyst at Boeing.

Alongside her work, she researches physics, propulsion and communication methods, and is a student at Bellevue College.
